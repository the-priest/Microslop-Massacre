# Microslop Massacre 2.14.1 (beta)

An open-world RPG in the world of *Mr. Robot*, played like *Fallout: New Vegas*. Free, original, built entirely in code.

## Download

- **Windows:** `MICROSLOP-MASSACRE-v2.14.1-beta-windows.zip`. Unzip anywhere and run `MicroslopMassacre.exe`.
- **Linux:** `MICROSLOP-MASSACRE-v2.14.1-beta-linux.zip`. Unzip, then `chmod +x MicroslopMassacre.x86_64` and run it.

No installer and no account. Saves go in your user folder.

## What's new

- **Quests come one at a time, from people.** A gold **!** over someone means they have work for you. Calls and texts arrive spaced out, and you answer them when you're ready (or later, in the journal: **J**). Nothing piles up because you walked into a room.
- **Smooth at any frame rate.** Cars, planes, boats and walking no longer judder or shake. The chase camera ignores lamp posts and glides back out, and cars lean into turns.
- **Boats** at eight docks. Sail from Chicago's lakefront into Redmont's reservoir, or out of Port Ramsey to Price Island.
- **Graffiti everywhere** in a real Bronx handstyle, plus 72 buffed walls across every map that you can paint yourself (ALL CITY).
- **Ragdolls and dismemberment.** Bodies fall the way the shot pushes them, and a big enough hit can take a limb off. New perk: Bloody Mess.
- **Sniper scopes** with a reticle, a rangefinder, breath-holding (**Shift**) and a second zoom step (mouse wheel).
- **Wildlife:** pigeons, gulls, stray dogs, deer and cows.
- **Easier to use things.** Doors, subway stairs, terminals and people respond from any angle.
- **Planes stay where you park them.** Prompts say whose plane it is and who to ask for the keys.
- The E Corp break-in now walks you through each step. Cops stand down once you've lost the heat.

## Known issues (it's a beta)

- Building collapses from plane crashes are in the code but switched off until they're finished.
- Let us know what breaks: open an issue saying what you were doing and on which map.
